# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

**生效日期 / Effective Date: 2026-10-22**

## 中文

「开封日历」（FreshDate，以下简称"本 App"）由开发者个人提供。我们非常重视您的隐私。本政策说明本 App 如何处理您的信息。

### 一、我们不收集任何数据

本 App **不收集、不存储、不传输**任何可识别您个人身份的信息，包括但不限于：

- 姓名、邮箱、电话号码等联系方式
- 位置信息
- 设备标识符、广告标识符（IDFA）
- 使用行为数据（除 Apple 系统级、由您自主选择是否分享的分析数据外）

本 App 不包含任何第三方统计、分析或广告 SDK。

### 二、您的数据存储在哪里

- 您录入的所有物品信息（名称、开封日期、提醒设置等）仅存储在**您的设备本地**。
- 如果您开启了 iCloud 同步，数据将通过 Apple 的 iCloud 服务在您的设备间同步，由 Apple 依据其隐私政策保护，开发者无法访问。
- 本 App 无需注册账号，没有我们自己的服务器。

### 三、内购

Pro 终身版为一次性买断，购买与支付流程由 Apple App Store 完成。我们不会接触您的支付信息。

### 四、通知权限

本 App 仅在您授权后，于物品临期或到期时在本地向您发送提醒通知。通知内容不会离开您的设备。

### 五、儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 13 岁以下儿童，亦不有意收集儿童信息。

### 六、政策更新

如本政策发生变更，我们将在本页面更新并注明生效日期。

### 七、联系我们

如对本隐私政策有任何疑问，请通过 App Store 页面上的开发者联系方式与我们联系。

## English

FreshDate ("the App") is provided by an independent developer. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

### 1. We Collect No Data

The App does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

- Contact information such as your name, email, or phone number
- Location data
- Device identifiers or advertising identifiers (IDFA)
- Usage or behavioral data (except Apple system-level analytics that you choose to share with developers)

The App contains no third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

### 2. Where Your Data Lives

- All item information you enter (names, opening dates, reminder settings, etc.) is stored **locally on your device**.
- If you enable iCloud sync, your data syncs across your devices via Apple's iCloud service, protected under Apple's privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.
- The App requires no account and operates no servers of its own.

### 3. In-App Purchase

Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase. All payment processing is handled by the Apple App Store. We never see your payment information.

### 4. Notifications

With your permission, the App sends local notifications when items are about to expire. Notification content never leaves your device.

### 5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.

### 6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and note the effective date.

### 7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the developer contact on the App Store page.
